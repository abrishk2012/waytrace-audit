# Demo video storyboard

## First 10 seconds — clip9, trip 14 (HELD-OUT, even)

| Time | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 s | clip9 from 14 s. Person walks in, then STOPS. Nothing else. | silence |
| 4-7 s | They move on. Cut to 24 s - they STOP AGAIN, same place. | silence |
| 7-10 s | Freeze on the second stop. One line of text. | narration starts |

Line at 7 s: **"Twice, nine seconds apart, in the same square metre."**

No logo. No title card. No music. The silence is the point.

## Why this clip
- Trip 14 is EVEN = held-out. Never used to tune anything.
- Both stops found by a detector that had never seen this clip.
- Both stops are in hotspot 2 - the one the dashboard leads with.
- Video and dashboard tell ONE story.

## Rest of the video
1. 0-10 s   problem, shown not told
2. 10-40 s  raw footage -> boxes, IDs, trails
3. 40-70 s  an event firing on screen
4. 70-100 s dashboard: hotspot map, signage finding
5. 100-120 s precision and recall + one honest limitation
